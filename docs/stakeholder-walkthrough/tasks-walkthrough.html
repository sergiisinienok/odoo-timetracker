    Ops · Priya, in Odoo

    Priya sets up the project and its tasks

    Northwind's project is billable by default. Priya adds the tasks people will log against. Each task either follows the project or overrides it.

    • Design and Development follow the project, so they are billed.
    • Rework and Ramp-up are set to not billable. This is where absorbed work goes.
    • The Internal project gets PTO, Bench, Training and Internal work. None of them are billed.
    Only ops creates tasks. Employees can't add one from the app. If a task is missing, they ask ops.

    Employee · Maria, in the app

    Maria logs a normal day

    Maria opens the app on 14 October. Her usual project is already selected, and the task is already set to Development, the last task she used on Northwind. She sets the hours, types a short note and saves.

    If she switches the project, the task changes to the one she last used on that project.

    Try it: change the project, then press Save.
    • Same number of taps as today.
    • No question about billing anywhere on the screen.

    Unpaid work goes on a task, not on a separate “(unpaid)” option

    For the room: Maria still makes a choice here. She chooses what kind of work it was, not whether the client pays.

    The month shows totals by project, then by task

    At the bottom of the month view, Maria sees where her time went. Today this list shows one total per client. After the change, each project lists its tasks underneath.

    Employee · Maria, in the app

    Lines logged before the change show “No task”

    Lines Maria logged before the change have no task. They show as No task and are otherwise untouched.

    If she edits one while its month is still open, she has to pick a task before she can save. She can still delete it without picking one. Lines in a closed month can't be edited at all, as today.

    Try it: press Edit on the 30 Sep line, then Save without choosing a task.

    Approver · Daniel, in Odoo

    The client agrees to pay for one rework line

    Maria logged three Rework lines this month. Northwind agrees that the 9 October one was a change of scope, so they will pay for it.

    Daniel opens Maria's timesheet in Odoo and sets that one line's Sales Order Item. That makes the line billed. The other two stay unbilled.

    Daniel picks the order line in the empty cell. The app has no screen for this. The approver does it in Odoo, where they already validate the month.

    Employee → Ops · Tom, then Priya

    A billable task with nothing to bill against

    Priya adds a Client workshop task to the Internal project and marks it billable by mistake. Tom logs 4 hours on it.

    A billed line has to point at a line on a sales order, and the Internal project has none. The app can't bill the line, but it doesn't block Tom over a setup problem he can't see or fix.

    • Tom sees a normal “Saved”.
    • The line is saved as not billed.
    • The next morning's digest tells ops, so they can fix the setup or ask the approver to set the line by hand.

    Owner / management

    What Northwind is billed for October

    Maria's billed hours reach the invoice through the sales order, as they do today. The only difference is which lines count: Development and Design, plus the one Rework line Daniel set by hand.

    Flat-rate clients pay the same monthly amount whatever the hours. For them, billed vs. not billed hours only show how much of the work was in scope and how much we absorbed.
